China's factory activity unexpectedly dropped in October, its third contraction in four months, a government survey showed on Saturday (Oct 31), hurt by soft demand for exports as well as goods at home. The official manufacturing purchasing managers' index (PMI) fell to 49.3 from 50.1 in September, according to data from the National Bureau of Statistics. ...
