[2026-10-19 12:32 UTC] Canada Consumer Price Index, September 2026  (impact: high)
URL: https://www.forexfactory.com/news/1432092-canada-consumer-price-index-september-2026
Source: statcan.gc.ca
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Consumer Price Index (CPI) rose 3.1% year over year in September, following a 3.0% increase in August. The acceleration was led by higher prices for gasoline in September (+27.8%) compared with August (+22.8%). Excluding gasoline, the CPI rose 2.2%, after increasing 2.4% in August. Moderating the acceleration in the all-items CPI in September were smaller year-over-year price increases for travel tours and groceries compared with the previous month. The CPI increased 0.1% month over month in September. On a seasonally adjusted monthly basis, the CPI rose 0.4%. Prices for energy accelerated in September, rising 19.0% year over year following a 15.4% gain in August. Crude oil prices increased in September, reaching their highest level since May amid ongoing uncertainty about the wars in the Middle East, resulting in higher prices for gasoline and fuel oil.CANADA INFLATION RISES TO 3.1%, BUT CORE PRESSURES EASE Canada's annual inflation accelerated to 3.1% in September, below the 3.2% forecast, as higher oil prices pushed energy costs up. Monthly CPI rose 0.1%, also below expectations. Meanwhile, the Bank of Canada's preferred core inflation measures averaged 1.85%, their lowest level in six years. The key takeaway: Energy is driving headline inflation higher, while underlying inflation continues to cool.
