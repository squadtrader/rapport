The Consumer Sentiment Index in the United States was largely unchanged in October, arriving at 48 points from the last month's 48.1 reading, according to the final report published by the University of Michigan on Friday. Still, the figure decreased by 10.4% compared to the same month last year, but still came above expectations. The Current Economic Conditions Index fell by 8.3% from September to 46.7, tumbling by 20.3% annually. Meanwhile, the Index of Consumer Expectations rose by 5.4% month-on-month to 48.8 but remained 3% below its October 2025 level. "Purchasing power continues to dominate consumer views of the economy," Surveys of Consumers Director Joanne Hsu said. She underscored that this month's decline in the Current Conditions subindex was driven entirely by buying conditions for durables, which plunged 18% on the basis of higher interest rates and rising concerns about the affordability of such purchases."US Univ.
