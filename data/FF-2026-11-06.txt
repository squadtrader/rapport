The uncollateralized overnight call rate had been in the range of 0.976 to 0.980 percent. Meanwhile, the Bank had conducted Japanese government bond (JGB) purchases of about 2.5 trillion yen per month in accordance with the plan for the outright purchases of JGBs decided at the June 2026 meeting. In the money market, the uncollateralized overnight call rate had been at around 1.0 percent. The liquidity indicators in the JGB markets continued to improve.
